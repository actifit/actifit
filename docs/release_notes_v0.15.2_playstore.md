# Actifit v0.15.2 — Play Store "What's New" (500 char limit)

First production release since v0.14.0, so it covers everything in v0.14.1–v0.15.2. (v0.15.0 and v0.15.1 never reached users. v0.15.2 replaces them, points the DHF vote prompt at proposal #391, and reads only steps from Health Connect.)

---

• The Arena: join fitness challenges, track your rank and earn AFIT
• Friends: requests, suggestions and search
• New profile with a companion that evolves as you move
• New dashboard rings for steps, estimated distance and calories
• Body metrics charts and shareable progress cards
• Set your own daily step goal
• AI assistant and AI workout plans
• Photo privacy: location and device details removed from uploads
• Health Connect and Fitbit fixes, 13 languages updated
