# Actifit v0.14.0 — Full Release Notes

## Dashboard Overhaul
- Completely redesigned dashboard with Material Design 3 cards and a cleaner layout
- New 7-day consistency streak strip to track your daily activity habit
- Monthly activity heatmap to visualize your fitness calendar
- Community feed preview showing recent posts from the Actifit community
- Revamped news carousel with tweet/social post slides including Like button interaction
- Improved reward nudge card tracking all reward tiers

## Health Connect
- New prominent banner when Health Connect permissions are needed — easy to dismiss and restore from the step card
- Step history charts and monthly heatmap now powered by parallel Health Connect data tables
- Fixed step ring disappearing when permissions are pending

## Route Map Tracking
- Track your GPS route during activities and view it on a map

## Post Screen
- Redesigned with a cleaner editor, improved tag input (space/comma/paste to add tags), and better toolbar

## Wallet
- Redesigned with Material Design 3 accordion sections
- New Hive Transactions section
- Hive Engine token list now scrollable; shows "No tokens found" when account has no tokens
- Fixed Hive Engine balance loading

## Backup & Restore
- New local backup and restore for your database and app preferences
- Fully localized across all 13 supported languages

## Achievement Flex Cards
- New visual achievement cards for sharing your fitness milestones on social media

## Post Image Carousel
- Enhanced with navigation arrows, dots indicator, and loading spinners for a smoother browsing experience

## Other Screens
- Login screen redesigned with Material Design 3
- Settings screen redesigned with MD3 card layout
- Leaderboard, social, market, and activity history screens improved

## Bug Fixes & Polish
- Replaced QR scanner with a device-only offline scanner (no Google ML Kit dependency)
- Fixed tutorial video opening in YouTube app instead of a broken in-app view
- Fixed several crashes on Android 16, during language changes, and on the discussions screen
- Post & Earn button now extends/shrinks on scroll
- Hive RPC fallback mechanism for improved blockchain reliability
- 13 languages updated with new string translations throughout
