# Actifit v0.14.0 (64) Release Notes

## Major Updates
*   **Material Design 3 Wallet**: A complete overhaul of the wallet UI, bringing a modern, intuitive experience for managing AFIT, HIVE, HBD, and other tokens.
*   **Health Connect 2.0**: Re-engineered integration with Google Health Connect, improving sync reliability and data accuracy.
*   **Enhanced Dashboard**:
    *   **Activity Heatmap**: Visualize your consistency over time.
    *   **Streak Tracking**: Monitor your consecutive active days.
    *   **Nudge Cards**: Smart notifications and reminders to keep you on track.
*   **AI Insights**: Integrated AI-powered analysis to provide personalized feedback on your fitness progress.
*   **Multi-Language Support**: Expanded localization to support more users globally.

## Improvements & Fixes
*   Updated `MainActivity` logic for better tracking source management (Phone, Fitbit, Health Connect).
*   Refined UI components for better alignment with Material Design 3 standards.
*   General performance optimizations and stability improvements.
