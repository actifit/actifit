# 🚀 Actifit Android v0.14.0 "Momentum" — Biggest Update Yet!

Hey Actifit community! 👋

We're thrilled to announce the release of **Actifit Android v0.14.0**, codenamed **"Momentum"** — and it lives up to the name. This is by far the most comprehensive update we've shipped, touching nearly every screen in the app with a modern redesign, powerful new features, and a long list of fixes and polish.

Let's dive in! 💪

---

## 🎨 Full Dashboard Overhaul

The dashboard has been completely rebuilt from the ground up with **Material Design 3**, bringing a cleaner, more modern look and feel that puts your fitness data front and center.

Here's what's new on your home screen:

- **7-Day Streak Strip** — A new consistency tracker showing your last 7 days at a glance. Stay on your streak and keep that momentum going! 🔥
- **Monthly Activity Heatmap** — Visualize your entire month of activity in a beautiful calendar grid. See your patterns, spot your gaps, and stay motivated.
- **Community Feed Preview** — A live strip of recent Actifit community posts right on your dashboard, keeping you connected to fellow fitness earners.
- **Upgraded News Carousel** — Now includes tweet/social slides with profile images, media backgrounds, and a Like button — all without leaving the app.
- **Smarter Reward Nudge Card** — Tracks all reward tiers and gives you the right nudge at the right moment to hit your next milestone.

---

## 🏃 GPS Route Map Tracking

You can now **track your GPS route** during activities and view your path on a map after your session. Whether you're running, cycling, or hiking — your route is recorded and displayed right inside Actifit. 🗺️

---

## 💚 Health Connect Improvements

We've significantly improved the Health Connect experience:

- **New Permission Banner** — Instead of a tiny buried icon, a clear banner now appears at the top of your dashboard when Health Connect permissions are needed. Dismiss it when you're not ready, and a small icon in the step card keeps the option accessible anytime.
- **Parallel Step History Tables** — HC step data now powers your history charts and monthly heatmap with dedicated data tables for faster, more reliable sync.
- **Step Ring Fix** — The step ring now stays visible while permissions are pending, so your dashboard never looks broken.

---

## 💼 Wallet Redesign

The wallet screen has been completely redesigned with **Material Design 3 accordion sections** for a cleaner, more organized layout:

- New **Hive Transactions** section to review your on-chain activity
- Hive Engine token list is now **scrollable** with a proper empty state when no tokens are found
- Fixed Hive Engine balance loading reliability
- Improved AFIT, HIVE, and HBD balance display

---

## ✍️ Post Screen Redesign

Composing your daily activity report is now smoother than ever:

- Cleaner editor layout with an improved toolbar
- **Smarter tag input** — tags now convert automatically on space, comma, or paste
- Better expand mode for distraction-free writing
- Post & Earn FAB extends and shrinks as you scroll

---

## 🏆 Achievement Flex Cards

New **visual achievement cards** let you celebrate and share your fitness milestones directly to social media. Hit a goal? Flex it! 💪📱

---

## 🖼️ Enhanced Post Image Carousel

Browsing posts in your feed is now much smoother:

- Navigation arrows and dots indicator for multi-image posts
- Loading spinners so you always know content is on the way
- Fixed image recycling artifacts that caused incorrect previews

---

## 💾 Backup & Restore

You can now **back up and restore** your Actifit database and app preferences locally — protecting your activity history, settings, and data even when switching devices. Available in all **13 supported languages**. 🌍

---

## ⚙️ Settings & Other Screens

- **Login screen** redesigned with Material Design 3
- **Settings screen** rebuilt with MD3 card layout for a much cleaner look
- **Leaderboard, Social, Market, and Activity History** screens all received UX improvements and bug fixes

---

## 🔧 Under the Hood

- **Offline QR Scanner** — Replaced Google ML Kit with a fully device-local ZXing scanner. No network dependency, faster and more private.
- **Hive RPC Fallback** — Improved blockchain reliability with automatic fallback to alternative nodes when the primary is unavailable.
- **Android 16 compatibility** — Fixed crashes on the latest Android release.
- **Tutorial video** now opens correctly in the YouTube app.
- **Dark mode** text and button fixes across multiple screens.
- Discussions modal crashes resolved.
- Reward claim button animation fixed.
- 13 languages refreshed with new translations throughout.
