# Actifit Android Endorphin: Best Update, Videos Back [Live]

Hey Actifitters! 💪

**Actifit Android v0.16.1 "Endorphin" is live on Google Play:** video reports are back with a new 3Speak upload, plus a home-screen steps widget, claimable badges, your Health Connect workouts on the dashboard, and a faster, smoother app.

Endorphin is the feel-good chemical your body releases when you move, and from now on every Android release gets a codename from your body's own chemistry. Next up: Adrenaline. 😉 This post covers everything new since v0.15.2. 👇

## 🎬 Actifit Video Reports Are Back

3Speak retired its old upload service. Because of that, we rebuilt video uploads from scratch on 3Speak's new one, with a brand-new video screen:

* **📤 Upload from your gallery or record right in the app**, with a live progress bar and a preview frame while it uploads.
* **🗂️ A redesigned "Your videos" screen:** thumbnails, durations, clear status chips (Processing, Ready, In a report) and a one-tap **Add** button.
* **⏱️ No waiting:** for example, you can add a video to your report while 3Speak is still processing it.
* **🖼️ Your video plays at the top of your report**, and a frame from it becomes the report's cover image.
* **🎯 Simple rules:** up to 10 uploads a day, one video per report, and short clips (under 2 minutes) work best.
* **🤝 Full transparency:** as 3Speak requires for videos on its network, a report with a video shares 11% of its post rewards with 3Speak (10% to the 3Speak fund, 1% for encoding).

---

## 📱 Your Steps, Right on Your Home Screen

Meet the new **Actifit steps widget**. Long-press your home screen, open Widgets, and add "Actifit steps":

* **⭕ Today's steps in a progress ring** toward your daily goal. Wider sizes also show steps vs. goal and your streak.
* **🔄 Always fresh:** in phone-sensor mode it updates about once a minute. With Health Connect it refreshes whenever the app syncs, and shows "Open Actifit to sync" instead of a misleading zero.
* **🌗 Follows your light or dark theme** and your in-app language. Tap it to jump straight into Actifit.

---

## 🏅 Actifit Badges, Now in the App

The badges you know from actifit.io are now on Android:

* **Profile → Badges:** ISO, Rewarded Activity levels 1 to 10, Lucky Doubled Up and Charity Donor.
* **Claim with one tap.** Badges you can claim are listed first, and tapping any badge tells you how to earn it.
* **Never miss one:** a "badges to claim" chip appears on your dashboard whenever a badge is waiting for you.

---

## 🏋️ Your Workouts From Health Connect

Turn on **Settings → Health Connect → Show my workouts** (it's optional) and Actifit shows the workouts you record in Google Fit, Samsung Health or your watch's app:

* **📋 A "Today's workouts" card** on your dashboard with each workout's type and minutes.
* **✍️ Faster reports:** syncing from Health Connect pre-selects the matching activity types and adds a workouts line to your report.
* **💡 Good to know:** workouts are shown for your record only. Rewards still come from your steps, and workouts you include are published with your public report.

---

## ✅ Fairer Rewards

* **👟 Only real steps count.** Steps recorded by your phone or tracker earn rewards. Steps typed into Health Connect by hand, however, no longer count, and a one-time note explains the change if it affects you.
* **🛰️ GPS-verified routes.** For recorded walks, runs and hikes, Actifit compares the steps counted during the route with the GPS distance. Matching routes get a **✓ GPS-verified** badge on the route map and in your report.

---

## 🔒 Better Privacy

* **🧠 Dashboard tips are now created on your phone**, which means your step data no longer leaves your device to make them.
* **🖼️ No broad photo access:** Actifit uses Android's own photo and video picker. As a result, it no longer asks for access to your whole gallery.

---

## ⚡ Faster and Smoother

* **🚀 Fewer freezes and a faster start:** step counting, database reads and startup work moved off the main screen thread.
* **🗺️ The route map opens instantly**, with no more multi-second freeze.
* **💰 A clearer earnings panel:** one card per token (AFIT, HIVE, BLURT, SPORTS) showing whether you're earning and why, plus how rewards work. The false "not earning" warnings are gone.
* **⏰ The daily post reminder works again**, and tags are cleaned to Hive's format.
* **↩️ Smoother back navigation** on newer Android versions.
* **🌍 Translation fixes** for Ukrainian, Yoruba and the Health Connect screens.

---

## ⌚ Fitbit Users: Switch to Health Connect

Google retired Fitbit's old connection for partner apps like Actifit on October 30, 2026. To keep earning from your Fitbit steps, turn on sharing with Health Connect in the Google Health (Fitbit) app, then switch Actifit to Health Connect. The app walks you through it.
