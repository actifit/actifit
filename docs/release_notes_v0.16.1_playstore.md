# Actifit v0.16.1 — Play Store "What's New" (500 char limit)

Covers everything since v0.15.2, the last production release (v0.16.0 was never shipped).

---

• New home-screen widget: today's steps, rings and your companion
• Badges: claim ISO, activity, lucky and charity badges from your profile
• See your Health Connect workouts on the dashboard and in reports (optional)
• Only automatically counted steps earn rewards
• Dashboard tips are now made on your phone
• GPS-verified badge for recorded routes; clearer earnings panel
• Videos are back: new 3Speak upload and a redesigned video screen
• Faster startup, fewer freezes, and translation fixes
